Privacy

How the Nanny-Delvin Rivers Trust prototype website handles analytics, technical information and future data collection.

What this website currently does

This is a public prototype website. It provides information about the Trust, catchment restoration, citizen science, nature-based solutions and related activities.

The current demonstration forms are not connected to a live submission system. They do not store or forward entries.

Cloudflare Web Analytics

The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand page use and website performance. Cloudflare describes this service as privacy-first and cookie-free, and states that it does not collect or use visitors' personal data or track individuals across customer websites.

The analytics beacon collects limited page-view and performance information. This helps assess which pages are being used and whether they load effectively.

Read Cloudflare's Web Analytics documentation.

Hosting and technical records

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub and network service providers may process technical request information required to deliver, secure and operate the site under their own terms and privacy notices.

Links to other websites

This site may link to external organisations, datasets or guidance. Their privacy practices are governed by their own notices. The Trust is not responsible for how an external website processes information after a visitor follows a link.

Future forms and services

Before any contact form, volunteer registration, citizen-science submission or mailing list becomes operational, the Trust will define the data controller, lawful basis, purpose, retention period, access controls and response process.

This privacy notice must be updated before such processing begins.

Your questions

Official privacy contact details will be added when the Trust's domain email and governance arrangements are operational.
