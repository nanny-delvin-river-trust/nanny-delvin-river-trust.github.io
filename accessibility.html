Accessibility

The accessibility aims, current status, known limitations and reporting route for the Nanny-Delvin Rivers Trust website.

Our accessibility aim

The Trust aims to make this website usable by as many people as reasonably possible, including people using keyboards, screen readers, browser zoom and small-screen devices.

Development is guided by semantic HTML, clear headings, descriptive links, readable contrast, responsive layouts and meaningful alternative text.

Current status

This website is an active prototype and has not yet undergone a formal independent accessibility audit.

The current development target is alignment with the relevant principles of WCAG 2.2 Level AA. This is an aim, not a claim of complete conformance.

Measures already in place

  • Responsive layouts for desktop and mobile screens
  • Semantic headings and page landmarks
  • Keyboard-accessible links and standard form controls
  • Labels associated with prototype form fields
  • Alternative text for substantive images
  • No automatic audio, animation or time-limited interaction

Known limitations

  • The site has not yet been tested across a full range of assistive technologies.
  • Some prototype content and long operational pages may require further simplification.
  • Third-party pages and documents linked from this site may have different accessibility standards.
  • Future maps, dashboards or live forms will require separate accessibility testing.

Reporting an accessibility problem

Official accessibility contact details will be added when the Trust's domain email is operational.

Until then, technical accessibility issues may be recorded through the public GitHub repository:

Open the repository issue tracker.

Please describe the page, the problem encountered, the browser or assistive technology used, and the format or adjustment that would help.

Review

This statement should be reviewed when major interactive features are introduced, after a formal accessibility audit, or when the Trust establishes its official contact route.
